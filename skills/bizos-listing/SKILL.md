# BizOS Listing

Create or improve Amazon Listing content from what is known about **this product** and what can actually be observed about its market. The result should remain useful when BizOS MCP is unavailable.

## Start with the task

Identify the requested marketplace and language, product or ASIN, output modules, intended buyer, and any existing Listing. Collect the user's own specifications, package contents, compatibility, certifications, and claim evidence. Ask only for missing facts that would materially change the requested copy; label other gaps in the draft.

## Acquire competitor and keyword evidence

Read [evidence paths](references/evidence-paths.md) when competitor, keyword, traffic, review, or market claims matter.

1. If relevant **BizOS MCP** tools are actually connected and authorized, query them for the requested marketplace and product/category. Use returned data only within its observed scope and timestamp. A configured connection or a tool name alone is not evidence of a successful query.
2. If those results are unavailable or incomplete, use user-provided ASINs, exports, and reviews, then accessible public competitor pages or search results where the host permits. Open the underlying page when its content supports a material conclusion. Do not claim access to sales, search volume, CPC, conversion, or private account performance from a public Listing page.
3. If neither path yields enough competitor evidence, continue with an own-facts-based draft or audit marked **provisional**. State which comparison or keyword decisions remain unverified and request the smallest useful missing input.

The two paths are complementary: fill specific gaps from the other path, but keep each source's identity, date, marketplace, and evidence quality visible. Do not silently treat a search snippet, third-party estimate, and first-party observation as equivalent.

## Build the Listing

Read [copy and audit rules](references/copy-and-audit.md) for generation, rewrite, audit, Search Terms, or creative briefs.

Separate own-product facts from competitor claims. Competitors can show buyer language, objections, category patterns, and possible gaps; they cannot prove this product's materials, performance, certification, warranty, or compatibility. Choose a positioning angle only as strongly as the available evidence supports.

Deliver the requested modules in the target marketplace's language. For a full Listing, include a readable title, bullets, description, Search Terms proposal, and A+/image direction when useful. For an audit, give concrete issues and a revised draft or diffs. Keep claims traceable to the own-product facts or mark them for verification. Do not copy competitor wording.

## Finish with evidence and limits

Include a short source table with mode (`bizos_mcp`, `user_material`, `public_research`, or `limited`), source/ASIN or URL, marketplace, observed date, and what each source actually supports. Distinguish observed facts, external estimates, inference, and missing information. State how gaps affect the strength of the proposed copy.

Check current official Amazon and local-market requirements when a policy or compliance conclusion matters; do not present a generic checklist as approval to publish. This skill drafts and audits only. It never submits or changes a live Amazon Listing without a separate explicit instruction and an available authorized publishing tool.
